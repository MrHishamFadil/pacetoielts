Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Pace to IELTS is a small, independent IELTS Academic practice site. We collect only what we need to run your practice and progress record.

What we store

  • Your name, email address and profile photo from Google sign-in.
  • Your practice answers, Writing submissions and Speaking recordings you choose to submit.
  • Your scores, tutor marks and feedback, study plan and homework.
  • Basic dates, such as when you were last active.

Why we store it

To save your progress, mark your work, give tutor feedback, set homework and show your results on your dashboard.

Where it is stored

Sign-in and data storage use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Who can see it

You and your tutor. We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising.

Your choices

You can ask to see or delete your account and data at any time by contacting your tutor. Requests are handled promptly.

Children

If you are under 16, please use this site with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
