Your tutor

Hisham AbdelFadil, your IELTS tutor

Experienced international school English teacher · Cambridge CELT-P · 14+ years teaching

Hisham has taught English for more than 14 years, including over a decade as a Senior English educator at an international school in Jeddah, and two years teaching adults and university students academic English, essay writing and academic reading. He holds a B.A. in English Language and Literature from Cairo University and a Saudi teaching licence from ETEC. His Cambridge training includes CELT-P, TKT and Train the Trainer, plus a TESOL certificate.

How he helps you grow

  • Diagnoses your weak skills first, using the same data-driven approach he leads as his school's assessment coordinator (Harvard Data Wise trained).
  • Builds a step-by-step plan for your target band, with clear feedback linked to the official band descriptors.
  • Gives detailed, rubric-based writing feedback, built on years of teaching essay writing.
  • Coaches speaking with structured practice, drawing on experience running oral interview assessments.
  • Supports slower and less confident learners with patient, structured remediation.
