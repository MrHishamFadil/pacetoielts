The real test at a glance

IELTS Academic: format and scoring

Listening

About 30 min

4 parts, 40 questions. Recordings are heard once.

Reading

60 min

3 passages, 40 questions. No extra transfer time.

Writing

60 min

Task 1: 150+ words (~20 min). Task 2: 250+ words (~40 min), worth twice as much.

Speaking

11–14 min

3 parts, face to face with an examiner.

Writing criteria

Task achievement (Task 1) / Task response (Task 2) · Coherence and cohesion · Lexical resource · Grammatical range and accuracy

Speaking criteria

Fluency and coherence · Lexical resource · Grammatical range and accuracy · Pronunciation

Scores are reported on a 0–9 band scale in whole and half bands (e.g. 6.5). Source: ielts.org. Our practice sets differ in size from the real test.
