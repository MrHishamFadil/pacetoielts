Why “Pace to IELTS”?

Your pace. Your band. One clear path.

A runner keeps a pace: a steady rhythm that gets them to the finish line. Pace to IELTS means moving steadily, at your own speed, toward your target band.

You start with a diagnostic that finds your level, then follow a personal study plan, step by step. No rushing, no guessing what to study next.
