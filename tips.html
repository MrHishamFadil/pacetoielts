Tips

Simple strategies for each skill and for test day

A simple weekly plan

  1. Monday: one reading passage, timed (20 minutes).
  2. Tuesday: one listening section, then learn 10 vocabulary words.
  3. Wednesday: Writing Task 1 (20 minutes).
  4. Thursday: speaking, record yourself answering Part 1 and one cue card.
  5. Friday: Writing Task 2 (40 minutes).
  6. Weekend: review your mistakes notebook.

Reading

  1. Skim the passage for its main idea before reading the questions.
  2. For True/False/Not Given, "Not Given" means the text says nothing either way.
  3. Answers usually follow the order of the text, except for matching headings.

Listening

  1. Read the questions before the recording starts and predict the type of answer (number, name, noun).
  2. Check spelling and word limits.
  3. Speakers often correct themselves; the second answer is usually the right one.

Writing

  1. Task 1: write an overview of the main trends; do not give opinions.
  2. Task 2: answer every part of the question and state your position clearly.
  3. Leave three minutes to check grammar and spelling.

Speaking

  1. Extend answers with a reason and an example.
  2. It is fine to pause briefly or ask the examiner to repeat a question.
  3. Use your one minute of Part 2 preparation to note keywords, not full sentences.

Test day

  1. Bring the same ID you used to register.
  2. Sleep well and arrive early.
  3. If one question is hard, move on and come back later.
