Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the site and how to use it

Is the practice free?

Yes. Everything on the Free practice page costs nothing.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is free without an account. Click Sign in with Google at the top to see your name and a personal progress record. We only use your name and profile photo for this.

Are these real IELTS questions?

No. All questions are original and written in the style of the Academic test. For official samples, visit ielts.org.

Will this guarantee my band score?

No site can guarantee a score. Regular, honest practice helps you improve your skills and confidence.

How should I listen without audio?

Ask a friend to read the transcript aloud, or paste it into a text-to-speech tool, and answer without looking at the text.

Can I get my writing checked?

See the study plan and compare your work with our model outlines.
