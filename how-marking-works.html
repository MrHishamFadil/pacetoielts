How tutor marking works

How your Writing and Speaking get a band and feedback

Reading and Listening are marked instantly. Writing and Speaking are marked by a real tutor, Hisham AbdelFadil, using the same four criteria that IELTS examiners use.

Writing: the four criteria

  • Task Achievement (Task 1) or Task Response (Task 2): how fully and relevantly you answer the task.
  • Coherence and Cohesion: how clearly you organise and link your ideas.
  • Lexical Resource: the range and accuracy of your vocabulary.
  • Grammatical Range and Accuracy: the range and accuracy of your grammar.

The four criteria are weighted equally. In the real test, Task 2 carries more weight than Task 1. Task 1 needs at least 150 words and Task 2 at least 250 words.

Speaking: the four criteria

  • Fluency and Coherence
  • Lexical Resource
  • Grammatical Range and Accuracy
  • Pronunciation

The four criteria are weighted equally, and your Speaking band is their average.

How it works here

  1. Sign in with Google and open Writing or Speaking.
  2. Submit your essay or record your answer.
  3. Your tutor gives a band for each criterion, plus comments on what to fix next.
  4. You see your marks and feedback on My progress.

Bands from this site are a tutor's estimate to guide your practice. They are not official IELTS results. Official criteria: ielts.org.
