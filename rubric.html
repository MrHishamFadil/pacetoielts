Understand the Writing criteria

A plain-English reading aid for the four criteria used to assess IELTS Writing.

Reading aid only — not the official descriptors and not a band predictor.

Task response

For Task 2, this concerns how fully you answer the question, whether your position is clear, and whether your main ideas are relevant and developed.

  • Answer every part of the question.
  • State a clear position when the task asks for one.
  • Develop each main idea with explanation and support.
  • Stay on topic from the introduction to the conclusion.

Coherence and cohesion

This concerns how easily a reader can follow your ideas and how logically they connect.

  • Use paragraphs with one clear central idea each.
  • Order ideas logically.
  • Use linking words naturally, without overusing them.
  • Make sure every paragraph supports your overall answer.

Lexical resource

This concerns your range and control of vocabulary.

  • Use precise vocabulary for the topic.
  • Avoid repeating the same words unnecessarily.
  • Choose words you can use accurately.
  • Check spelling and word formation.

Grammatical range and accuracy

This concerns the variety and accuracy of your grammar.

  • Use a mix of simple and complex sentences where appropriate.
  • Check subject–verb agreement, tenses and articles.
  • Prioritise clear, accurate sentences over complicated ones.
  • Correct punctuation helps the reader follow your meaning.

Important note

This page is a plain-English study aid. It is not the official IELTS Writing band descriptors, does not award a band score, and cannot predict an official IELTS result. For official assessment information, refer to IELTS and British Council resources.
