    Understand the Writing criteria

    Reading aid only — not the official descriptors and not a band predictor.

    Task response

    • Answer every part of the question.
    • State a clear position when required.
    • Develop relevant main ideas with explanation and support.

    Coherence and cohesion

    • Use paragraphs with one clear central idea.
    • Order ideas logically.
    • Use linking words naturally.

    Lexical resource

    • Use precise topic vocabulary.
    • Avoid unnecessary repetition.
    • Check spelling and word formation.

    Grammatical range and accuracy

    • Use a range of sentence types accurately.
    • Check tenses, agreement and articles.
    • Prioritise clear meaning over complexity.
