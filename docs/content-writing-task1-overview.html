Task 1: Overviews & Key Features

Under official IELTS Academic Writing criteria, your Task 1 response cannot score above Band 5 in Task Achievement without an overview. Learn how to identify macro trends, select key features, and write clear overviews that satisfy the official band descriptors.

Official Band Descriptors: The Overview Rule

  • Band 7: "Presents a clear overview of main trends, differences or stages."
  • Band 6: "Presents an overview with information appropriately selected."
  • Band 5: "Presents no overview; data is presented as a list of facts."

Source: IELTS Scoring in Detail (ielts.org) [VERIFIED]

Drill 1: Identifying a Band 7+ Overview

Read the four sample overviews below based on the Clean Water Access graph. Decide whether each statement is an acceptable overview or flawed. Write ACCEPTABLE or FLAWED.

  1. "Overall, in 2010 the Northern Territories stood at 88%, while Riverlands was 55%, Coastal Plain was 42%, and Highland Valleys was 25%. By 2025, Northern Territories rose to 92%."
  2. "Overall, clean water access improved across three of the four regions over the fifteen-year period, with the Highland Valleys registering the most dramatic expansion. In contrast, the Coastal Plain was the sole region to experience a steady decline."
  3. "Overall, clean water infrastructure expanded because governments invested heavily in rural pipe networks and water purification technology."
Answers and Explanations
  1. FLAWED: This paragraph lists individual figures instead of summarizing general trends. Quoting specific statistics in the overview is a classic Band 5 error.
  2. ACCEPTABLE: Presents a clear general trend (three regions rose, one fell), highlights the key contrast (Highlands rose fastest, Coastal Plain was the only decline), and contains zero data numbers.
  3. FLAWED: This introduces outside speculation and causal claims. IELTS Task 1 asks only for information presented in the graphic; never invent reasons not supported by the data.

Drill 2: Selecting Key Features

Selecting key features means choosing the overarching contrasts that define the chart. Answer each question based on the visual data. Write ONE letter (A, B, or C).

  1. Which region maintained the highest access rate across the entire 15-year period? A) Riverlands, B) Northern Territories, C) Highland Valleys
  2. Which region showed the steepest rate of improvement? A) Highland Valleys, B) Riverlands, C) Northern Territories
  3. What was unique about the Coastal Plain compared to all other regions? A) It was always lowest, B) It remained completely unchanged, C) It was the only region to decrease
Answers and Explanations
  1. B: The Northern Territories began highest (88%) and finished highest (92%), remaining dominant throughout.
  2. A: The Highland Valleys surged from 25% to 71%, nearly tripling its initial proportion and climbing faster than any other region.
  3. C: While the other three regions experienced upward trajectories, the Coastal Plain registered a steady downward trend from 42% to 31%.

Drill 3: Framing Vocabulary for Overviews

Complete each sentence with ONE suitable academic word to form a cohesive overview. Write the word in lowercase.

  1. [___________], it is readily apparent that household water access increased in most regions over the monitored timeline. (Opening signpost)
  2. While access expanded in three areas, the Coastal Plain experienced a noticeable [___________]. (Synonym for decline / decrease)
  3. Northern Territories maintained a [___________] position throughout the fifteen years. (Adjective meaning leading / dominant)
Answers and Explanations
  1. overall: Standard academic discourse marker used to open the strategic overview paragraph.
  2. decline / downturn / decrease / reduction: Noun indicating the negative trajectory of the Coastal Plain.
  3. dominant / leading / commanding: High-level academic adjective describing the top position throughout the timeframe.
Model Answer & Rubric Commentary

Task 1 Model Report

Prompt: The line graph illustrates the percentage of households with reliable clean water access in four regions (Northern Territories, Riverlands, Highland Valleys, and Coastal Plain) between 2010 and 2025. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

The line graph compares the proportion of households with reliable access to clean water across four geographic areas from 2010 to 2025.

Overall, clean water availability improved in three out of the four surveyed regions over the fifteen-year period. Furthermore, while the Northern Territories consistently recorded the highest percentages, the Highland Valleys experienced the most substantial growth. In sharp contrast, the Coastal Plain was the sole region where access diminished.

In 2010, the Northern Territories started with a dominant 88% of households connected to clean water. This figure grew marginally but steadily, reaching 92% by 2025. Riverlands initiated the timeframe at 55% and climbed steadily to finish at 78% in the final year.

Conversely, the Highland Valleys witnessed the most striking transformation. In 2010, only one-quarter (25%) of homes had access; however, this proportion nearly tripled over the subsequent decade and a half, reaching 71% by 2025 and converging closely with Riverlands. By comparison, the Coastal Plain began at 42% but experienced a progressive downward trend, dwindling to 31% by the end of the period.

Examiner Rubric Commentary

CriterionHow this response satisfies the official descriptors
Task Achievement Includes an effective 3-sentence overview highlighting overall direction, the fastest-growing region, and the sole declining region without citing numbers. The body paragraphs group related categories and cite accurate key figures to support comparisons.
Coherence and Cohesion Clear four-paragraph structure. Smooth paragraph sequencing and natural cohesive referencing ("This figure", "Conversely", "over the subsequent decade and a half", "By comparison") without mechanical repetition.
Lexical Resource Precise academic vocabulary ("dominant", "converging closely", "striking transformation", "dwindling", "progressive downward trend"). Correct numerical expressions ("nearly tripled", "one-quarter").
Grammatical Range and Accuracy Combines complex comparative clauses ("while X recorded..., Y experienced..."), participial phrases ("reaching 71% and converging closely"), and accurate passive/active aspect with zero prepositional slips.
