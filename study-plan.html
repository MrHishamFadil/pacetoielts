4-week study plan

A clear path from first practice to test day

About 45 minutes a day, five days a week. Adjust it to your own pace.

Week 1: Foundations

  1. Read all lessons.
  2. Reading Passage 1 untimed.
  3. Listening Sections 1 and 2.
  4. Learn vocabulary words 1–25.
  5. Speaking Part 1: two topics, recorded.

Week 2: Build accuracy

  1. Reading Passage 2 with the timer.
  2. Listening Sections 3 and 4.
  3. Writing Task 1.1 and 1.2.
  4. Vocabulary 26–50.
  5. Speaking cue cards 1–5.

Week 3: Exam conditions

  1. Reading Passage 3 timed.
  2. Listening Sections 5 and 6.
  3. Writing Task 2: three essays in 40 minutes each.
  4. Vocabulary 51–75.
  5. Speaking Part 3: two topics.

Week 4: Review

  1. Redo your lowest-scoring sections on My progress.
  2. Writing: one Task 1 and one Task 2 together in 60 minutes.
  3. Vocabulary 76–100.
  4. Full speaking practice with a friend.
  5. Read the test-day tips.
